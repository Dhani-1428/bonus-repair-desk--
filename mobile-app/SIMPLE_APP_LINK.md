### Step 2: Open on Your Phone

1. **Open Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android)** on your phone
2. **Type the link** in the address bar
3. **Press Enter**

That's it! Your website opens like an app!

### Step 3: Add to Home Screen (Make it Like an App)

**On iPhone:**
1. Tap the **Share button** (square with arrow)
2. Tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
3. Tap **"Add"**
4. Now you have an app icon on your home screen!

**On Android:**
1. Tap the **Menu** (3 dots)
2. Tap **"Add to Home Screen"** or **"Install App"**
3. Tap **"Add"** or **"Install"**
4. Now you have an app icon on your home screen!
